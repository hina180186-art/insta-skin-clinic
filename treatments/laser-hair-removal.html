Laser Physics

How Triple-Wavelength Destroys Hair Bulbs

Different hair strands sit at different skin depths. Our system fires three complementary wavelengths simultaneously for comprehensive clearance:

755

Alexandrite 755nm

Delivers powerful energy absorption by melanin chromophores, making it ideal for the most superficial, fine, and thin hair strands on the upper lip, chin, and eyebrows.

808

Classic Diode 808nm

The classic wavelength with deep tissue penetration that targets the mid-dermal follicle bulge. Perfect for dense body hair on arms, legs, back, and underarms.

1064

Nd:YAG 1064nm

Characterized by lower melanin absorption, making it the safest wavelength for tanned and rich Pakistani skin tones. Reaches deep follicle papillae without surface heating.

Pre-Treatment Guidelines

  • Shave Area 24 Hours Prior: Shave with a fresh razor. Do not wax, tweeze, or bleach for 4 weeks before laser.
  • Avoid Active Sun Exposure: Do not tan or sunbathe for 7 days prior to prevent melanin reactivity.
  • Clean Product-Free Skin: Arrive with skin clean of perfumes, heavy oils, or body deodorants.
2

Post-Treatment Recovery

  • Apply SPF 50+ Broad Spectrum: Protect treated zones daily with physical mineral sunscreen.
  • Avoid Heat for 24 Hours: Skip steam saunas, intense cardio workouts, and boiling showers for 24 hours.
  • Shedding Phase (Day 7–14): The treated hair will naturally fall out over the subsequent 10–14 days.
