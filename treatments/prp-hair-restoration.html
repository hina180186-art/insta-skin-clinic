Trichological Science

The Regenerative Cascade Explained

Androgenetic alopecia causes hair follicles to miniaturize under DHT attack until they stop producing visible shafts. PRP halts this cycle biologically:

1

Sterile Centrifugation

A small sample (15-20ml) of your blood is drawn in a sterile gel tube and spun in a precision medical centrifuge to isolate the gold platelet plasma from red blood cells.

2

Targeted Micro-Injections

Using ultra-fine, painless 32-gauge needles, the platelet concentrate is injected at 1.5mm–2mm depth directly around hair bulb roots in the thinning vertex and frontal hairline.

3

Follicle Awakening

Growth factors stimulate angiogenesis (new capillary networks) around follicles, restoring oxygen and nutrients to reverse miniaturization and trigger the Anagen phase.

1

Pre-PRP Protocol

  • Hydrate Abundantly: Drink at least 2 liters of water 24 hours prior to maximize plasma yield and quality.
  • Avoid Blood Thinners / NSAIDs: Refrain from Aspirin, Ibuprofen, and green tea supplements 48 hours before session.
  • Wash Scalp on Appointment Day: Arrive with freshly washed hair free of sprays, hair waxes, or fiber powders.
2

Post-PRP Protocol

  • Keep Scalp Dry for 24 Hours: Do not wash your hair or wet your scalp until 24 hours post-procedure.
  • Avoid Intense Sweating: Skip heavy gym workouts and swimming pools for 48 hours to prevent infection.
  • No Harsh Chemical Treatments: Delay hair coloring, bleaching, or rebonding treatments for 7 days.
