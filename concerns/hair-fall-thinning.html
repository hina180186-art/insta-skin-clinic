Clinical Diagnosis

Why Are You Losing Hair?

Accurate treatment requires isolating the root physiological mechanism behind follicle starvation:

Androgenetic Alopecia (DHT)

Genetic sensitivity to Dihydrotestosterone (DHT) binds to receptors in frontal and vertex follicles, causing gradual shrinking until strands become invisible peach fuzz.

Nutritional & Ferritin Deficits

Extremely prevalent in South Asian women. Low serum Ferritin (iron storage below 50 ng/mL) and Vitamin D3 deficiency trigger acute telogen effluvium, causing diffuse handfuls of hair to shed.

PCOS & Postpartum Hormones

Hormonal swings following childbirth or caused by Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome disrupt the normal 3-year growth cycle, pushing 30%+ of scalp follicles into premature resting phases.

Evidence-Based Medicine

Medical Science vs. Commercial Oil Myths

What Fails: Parlor Oils & Home Masks

Commercial hair oils, herbal pastes, and onion juices sit purely on the hair cuticle shaft. They cannot penetrate 3–4mm beneath the scalp to reach living follicular dermal papillae where cellular division occurs. Furthermore, heavy oils frequently clog sebaceous glands, triggering seborrheic dermatitis and worsening hair fall.

What Works: Clinical Regenerative Medicine

Autologous PRP directly introduces high concentrations of bioactive growth factors (PDGF, VEGF, TGF-beta) into the living root sheath. This stimulates new micro-vascularization (angiogenesis), re-opens biological nutrition pathways, and commands the follicle stem cells to enter vigorous Anagen growth.

Patient Guidance

Hair Loss FAQs

Shedding between 50 to 100 strands per day is a natural part of the hair cycle. However, if you notice more than 150 strands on your pillow, shower drain, or brush, or if you can see your scalp shining through under indoor lights, medical intervention should begin promptly before follicles scar over.
Hard water contains high mineral deposits of calcium and magnesium that form a film on hair strands, making them brittle and prone to snapping. While it does not kill the internal root itself, it weakens hair shafts drastically. Our doctors provide specific shower filtration and chelating scalp rinses as part of your care protocol.
We routinely evaluate Serum Ferritin (iron reserve), Complete Blood Count (CBC), Serum Vitamin D3, Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH), and for women with irregular cycles, free Testosterone and DHEA-S. You can bring existing reports to your consultation or our clinic will advise on local diagnostic labs.
