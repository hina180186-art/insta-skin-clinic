How We Treat Different Acne Scar Types

Acne scars vary in morphological depth. Our Gujranwala clinic uses multi-modal doctor protocols combining laser, subcision, and growth factors:

Rolling Scars & Wavy Texture

Caused by fibrotic bands tethering skin to subcutaneous tissue. We perform cannula subcision to sever the bands, followed by Fractional CO2 laser to stimulate collagen filling.

Boxcar Scars & Crater Pores

Characterized by sharp vertical edges. The high-energy fractional beam vaporizes the abrupt borders, feathering them smoothly into the surrounding epidermal surface.

Icepick Scars & Deep Pits

Narrow, deep columns extending into the reticular dermis. Treated with targeted focal TCA CROSS chemical reconstruction combined with fractional laser smoothing.

Pre-Laser Protocol

  • Stop Retinoids & AHAs (7 Days Prior): Cease Retin-A, Tretinoin, glycolic acid, and harsh acne scrubs 1 week prior.
  • No Active Sun Tanning: Strictly avoid direct midday sun or chemical peeling 2 weeks before session.
  • Prophylactic Care: Patients with a history of cold sores receive anti-viral prophylaxis from the doctor.
2

Post-Laser Recovery Protocol

  • Apply Prescribed Occlusive Healing Balm: Keep skin continuously hydrated with doctor-prescribed post-laser barrier cream.
  • Never Pick or Scratch Micro-Crusts: Microscopic bronze dots shed naturally by Day 4–5. Forcing them causes pigment marks.
  • Strict SPF 50+ Sun Protection: Sun protection is vital for 4 weeks post-treatment to avoid PIH rebound on brown skin.
